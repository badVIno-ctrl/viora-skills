Questions people actually ask

Does it publish without me?

Never. A draft sits until two people approve it, and you can require a specific person for any repository. Scheduled publishing still needs the approvals first.

What happens to a note when a release is rolled back?

The note is marked as rolled back on your changelog with the time and the reason you give. Nothing is silently deleted, because your customers already read it.

Can I keep writing notes by hand?

Yes, and Halyard learns from those. Hand-written notes are the training set for your voice, so the more you write, the closer drafts land.

Where does our code go?

Diffs are read in memory to produce the draft and are not stored. What we keep is the note, the approvals and the references. Details in security.
