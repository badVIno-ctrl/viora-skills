Component states

Eight states of one component, rendered at once. Companion classes stand in for the pseudo-classes a static page cannot trigger: the component CSS must target .is-hover, .is-focus and .is-active next to :hover, :focus-visible and :active.

1 default
2 hover .is-hover
3 focus .is-focus
4 active .is-active
5 disabled

Disabled still needs 3:1 against the surface, and a reason nearby.

6 loading

The label keeps its box, so nothing around it moves.

7 error

The message says what to do, not that something went wrong.
